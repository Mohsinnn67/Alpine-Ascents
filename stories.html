08 / TEAMWORK

Stories of shared achievement

Expedition success is collective. These accounts recognize visible milestones while remembering the many people whose work makes climbing possible.

NEPAL · 1953

A summit reached together

On a British-led Everest expedition, Tenzing Norgay and Edmund Hillary reached the summit. The ascent followed years of attempts and relied on the expertise and labor of a large team, including Sherpa climbers and high-altitude workers.

NEPAL · 1975

Opening a path for women

Junko Tabei reached Everest as part of the Japanese Women's Everest Expedition. Her achievement challenged barriers in a sport where women had often been excluded from major expeditions.
