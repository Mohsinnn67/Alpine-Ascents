06 / MILESTONES

Notable mountaineering records

These milestones are part of a much larger story of local knowledge, teamwork, and repeated attempts. Records can change as measurement and recognition evolve.

8,848.86 m

Highest mountain above sea level

Mount Everest, on the Nepal–China border. The two governments announced this jointly recognized elevation in 2020.

2020
1953

First confirmed summit of Everest

Tenzing Norgay and Edmund Hillary reached the summit on 29 May during a British-led expedition supported by a large team.

EVEREST
1954

First ascent of K2

Italian climbers Lino Lacedelli and Achille Compagnoni reached the summit of the world’s second-highest mountain on 31 July.

KARAKORAM
1975

First woman to summit Everest

Junko Tabei of Japan became the first woman to reach Everest’s summit on 16 May as part of a Japanese women's expedition.

EVEREST

Elevation and historical records are summarized for learning; verify details with recognized mountaineering and geographic organizations.

Records celebrate milestones, but they do not measure the value of a climb or the contributions of every person involved.
