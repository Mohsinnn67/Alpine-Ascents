11 / BEFORE YOU GO

General mountain guidelines

Use these as a starting point, not a substitute for formal instruction, route-specific planning, or professional medical advice.

01

Choose a suitable objective

Match route difficulty, altitude, and exposure to the team's experience and current fitness. Read recent reports and check permits and access rules.

02

Make a plan and share it

Set a route, turnaround time, weather limits, and alternatives. Leave the plan and overdue procedure with a trusted contact.

03

Pack for the conditions

Carry navigation, suitable layers, water and food, first aid, headlamp, emergency shelter, and activity-specific equipment. Test your gear before departure.

04

Respect the team and the place

Communicate honestly, move at a sustainable pace, follow local guidance, minimize waste, and protect wildlife and fragile terrain.

05

Turn around early when needed

Weather, time, fatigue, illness, or uncertainty can make the summit no longer appropriate. A timely retreat is a sign of sound mountaineering judgment.

THE ESSENTIALS

Before leaving home

  • Route and latest conditions checked
  • Forecast and turnaround time agreed
  • Emergency contact and plan shared
  • Navigation and headlamp tested
  • Layers, food, water, first aid packed
  • Permits and local guidance confirmed
